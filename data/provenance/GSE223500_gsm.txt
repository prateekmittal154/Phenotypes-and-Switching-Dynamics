After this step nuclei were extracted by vigorous pipetting in salt-tris buffer with Tween-20 (TST)  followed by a high-volume wash in salt-tris buffer (ST). Nuclei were counted and 15,000 nuclei were separated for droplet-based RNA capture and reverse transcription using 10x Genomics  5' v1.1 or 5' V2.0  reagents and a Chromium controller.

!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Single-nuclei suspension from frozen NSCLC metastases were generated using methods published in Slyper et al. (Nature Medicine, 2020) with modifications. The tissue was first cut in 20 µm sections using a cryostat and stored on dry ice until the next step. Then the tissue was put on wet ice and washed once with 5 ml PBS to remove OCT. After this step nuclei were extracted by vigorous pipetting in salt-tris buffer with Tween-20 (TST)  followed by a high-volume wash in salt-tris buffer (ST). Nuclei were counted and 15,000 nuclei were separated for droplet-based RNA capture and reverse transcription using 10x Genomics  5' v1.1 or 5' V2.0  reagents and a Chromium controller. 
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Chromium Single Cell V(D)J Enrichment Kit for human T cells Reagent Kit, v 1.1 or Single Cell Human TCR Amplification Kit were utilized for snTCR library generation (10X genomics) depending on whether 5' V1 or 5' V2 kits were utilized for cDNA library generation, respectively. Final sequencing libraries were prepared using Single Index Kit T Set A or Library construction kit (catalog no. 1000190) and Dual Index Kit TT set A (catalog no. 1000215).
